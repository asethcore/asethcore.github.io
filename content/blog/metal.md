hello there dear fellers! hope all is good with y’all. this blog is kinda an update blog on what has been going on in my life. i’ll keep it short cause no one asked for this and no one is particularly interested in someone else's life unless it falls in line with theirs.

so, i have once again hopped on the nixos train, second try, i’d say. i still don't think i am using it as it's intended to be used. i’m using it like i use just any other distro. before this, i was using fedora because it kinda feels like home. i’ve tried quite a few distros and fedora has always been the one i come back to. well, there are some things i find troublesome there as well, but troubles are everywhere, i guess. one of the problems i had was that some of the stuff i wanted to use wasn't available in the official repos. i know, i know, copr exists, but for the most part i am under the impression that it's not as in-sync as things in the official repo (i've experienced it, that's why i say so, though i might be wrong). now, i could have installed the nix package manager on fedora and installed things from there, but well… things didn't go as i wanted and i gave up (i'm not really experienced with all this stuff).

here is the pic of my setup right now. of course, the fetch is mandatory.

like i said, i’m not using nixos like people normally do. i just use it as i use any other distro, but i’m learning. thankfully nothing has broken so far (mostly because i haven't made much progress).

on another note, i joined a team that wanted to participate in a competition, and spoiler warning: it was a bad decision. i shouldn't have agreed to it. the team members themselves aren't bad at all, they’re decent people, but the team formation was complete arse. firstly, the people organizing it had no idea what they wanted to work on, and then they scheduled like 4 or 5 meetings just to figure that out. it became so fucking annoying that i started ignoring them for a while, which was bad on my end. so, i made up my mind to just stick along until it finishes, and then i won't ever talk about it again.

anyways, quite a few things are going through my head right now. mostly, i'm just hoping, hoping for things to not suck for me, i guess. every day i wake up, i hope to see her, but my hope fails to meet reality. it doesn't sadden me or anything; it’s just that the hope gets to live one more day, and as the days extend, it won't let me forget, would it?

writing is something i usually do as an exit for the thoughts in my mind. so, i wrote a letter to her. except it isn't meant to be sent, it will just stay with me so that someday i might read it and laugh. i sometimes don't know what my mind has developed into. i can't stop thinking nowadays. it's just endless thoughts, none of them random at all. each one feels like an exit that leads me out of reality and brings me right back to it once explored. i don't let go of any thought, there has to be a complete autopsy of it.

i used to call it overthinking, because that's what it was. back then, my thoughts never led me to an exit from reality, but now they do. there is a white room in my mind, a room that i own completely. there is a mushy, deep red couch in it (not a bright red), and i am sitting there in black attire, reading something. there is also a counter with a few chairs. i don't know why i have multiple chairs when it's only me there, but i guess that's just how i like it. there is also a refrigerator, empty, with nothing inside.(i think i am mentall unwell)

<img src="/blogs/images/metal/white-room.jpeg" alt="sunchild" class="post-image"/>

i think a lot nowadays, or should i say, there are a lot of exits. i’m not actively looking to use them, but they’re there in case i ever want to. honestly, i'm kinda happy or satisfied with whatever reality is right now. it's mostly acceptance. i have this restless mind and i don't know how to be conscious without a constant stream of thought, i have no clue what that even feels like. if there is something i'd want to know, it's how it feels to be inside someone else's mind and experience their thought process. not a specific person, just anyone.

i don't want it as an escape from myself, but as an experience. though i do believe we sometimes need a break from being ourselves... or do we? i don't know. some might argue that if you need a break from being yourself, what are you even retreating to? and if you are retreating to something else, then you aren't who you think you are. but to me, retreating just means stepping away from personal matters for a bit to look at things again. yo no sé. i feel like every time you really look at something, you notice something you missed before, or something you forgot. then again, maybe that isn't taking a break from yourself, that’s just adjusting the zoom. you don't want to zoom out too much or you'll lose focus of yourself and everything will feel like a cliché. just being aware is enough, rather than being completely submerged in it. i know this because i think i was close to it once.

i know how it feels when hope finally aligns with reality, when hope is no longer just hope, but actual reality. i have experienced it firsthand. it feels unexpected at first, and then as your gaze settles on it, it blooms like a spotlight. of course, the spotlight isn't on you or me, but just to be sure that what’s in front of you is real and not an illusion, you double check it, triple check it. it’s so much joy, yet never enough that you can't take it all in. it’s beautiful, like a golden grace.

lingering somewhere in my mind, there was more that i hoped i could have done, but i guess only one thing at a time. i keep playing it over and over in my head, and even though that thought of "being more" is still there, it doesn't reduce or affect the beauty of that moment in any way. i will keep on hoping, but never fill myself with it.

“why not try it all if you only remember it once?” — the strokes (i’ll try anything once)
